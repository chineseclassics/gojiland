# Child Safety Baseline

GojiLand is publicly viewable but family-managed.

## First-release rules

- Visitors do not create accounts.
- Visitors do not publish content.
- There are no comments, chat, ratings, direct messages, or public profiles.
- Goji is identified by first-name nickname and age only with parent approval.
- Do not publish school, class, timetable, location, contact details, private
  photos, voice recordings, or identifying documents.
- Do not install advertising or behavioural tracking.
- Do not request camera, microphone, screen capture, precise location,
  Bluetooth, USB, or notification permissions without a separate review.

## Reviews required before expansion

A privacy and moderation review is required before adding:

- user accounts or email collection
- analytics tied to individuals
- uploads, photos, audio, or free-form text
- multiplayer or communication
- AI features that send children's content to a third party
